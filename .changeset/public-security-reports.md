Ask security reports to open a public issue and email the maintainer

The `SECURITY.md` template in `project-templates` no longer routes vulnerability reports through
GitHub's private advisory flow. Reporters open a public `[SECURITY]` issue and email the same
report to the maintainer, so the discussion stays on the public record. Private repositories
still drop the issue step and keep the email contact only.
